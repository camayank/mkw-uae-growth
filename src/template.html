The rules behind the tool

Who must do what, by when

Revenue means gross income in the most recent accounting period per your financial statements (Ministerial Decision 244 of 2025, as amended by Decision 66 of 2026). B2C-only businesses are excluded until the Minister sets a date. VAT registration does not matter: unregistered businesses are in scope and must obtain a TIN. Free zones, designated zones, DIFC and ADGM are all in scope.

Penalties (Cabinet Decision 106 of 2025)

The Ministry's October 2026 programme deck refers to a Cabinet Decision 173 of 2026 on penalties and marks Decision 106 as repealed; its text was not published anywhere we could find on 17 Oct 2026. Treat these amounts as the last published schedule and expect changes.
