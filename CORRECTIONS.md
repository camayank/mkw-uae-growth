# Corrections policy

The selector and the Market Map describe each accredited provider from its own public website, graded by how much the site confirmed. Nothing is inferred and no provider pays for inclusion, ranking or description.

**Providers:** if your record is out of date, open a "Listing correction" issue (template provided) or email connect@mkwadvisors.com with the public page that proves the change. Corrections with a proving page are applied within two business days, dated in the record's sources, and shipped in the next build. We do not change a record on assertion alone, and no payment changes any data point or rank.

**Everyone else:** errors in the regulatory facts (dates, penalties, scope) are corrected the same way; please cite the Ministry of Finance, Federal Tax Authority or UAE Legislation portal page.
