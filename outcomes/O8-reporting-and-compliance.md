# O8 Reporting and compliance calendar

Status: verified-pending-merge (branch `outcome/O8-reporting-and-compliance`, stacked on O6, 17 Oct 2026)

## Business result

The founder sees the business in numbers that match the database: revenue and VAT from issued invoices,
cash collected, payouts due and paid, margin (finance and founder only), request-to-acceptance rate,
on-time rate and partner share by category, for any month, quarter or year. Each partner sees its own jobs,
on-time rate, payouts and scorecard trend. Finance exports payments, payouts, VAT and (finance only) margin
as CSV the accounting system can import. Every client has a compliance calendar of recurring obligations
(licence renewals, visa expiries, VAT and corporate tax returns, UBO, ESR) with reminders; an obligation
counts as met only when filing evidence is on file and MKW has checked it, and a statutory due date reaches
a client only after a person has confirmed it.

## Scope

1. **Founder dashboard** (`GET /api/admin/dashboard?period=2026-10|2026-Q4|2026`): figures computed in SQL
   from invoices, payments, payouts, requests, offers, engagements and milestones for the period, with the
   previous period for comparison. Margin figures only for finance and founder (ops gets the dashboard
   without them: same rule as everywhere). Pure helpers in `packages/core/src/reporting.ts` (period
   parsing and boundaries, rates). Screen: Dashboard for MKW staff.
2. **Partner dashboard** (`GET /api/partner/dashboard`): the firm's jobs by status, on-time rate, payouts
   paid / eligible / pending, scorecard trend (O6 rows). Nothing about other firms or clients' prices.
3. **Accounting export** (`GET /api/admin/accounting/export?from&to&set=sales|payouts|margin`): CSV, one row
   per issued tax invoice and credit note (net, VAT, disbursements, gross, client, number, date), per
   payment received and refunded, per payout released (partner, run, invoice number), and, for finance and
   founder only, margin per engagement line. Text fields are formula-safe; every export is audited with the
   range and row count. Column layout is the generic one; the owner's accounting system (Zoho Books, Xero,
   QuickBooks) decides the final mapping and is an owner decision, recorded as UNCERTAIN in the screen.
4. **Compliance calendar.** `obligations` table per client: kind (`licence_renewal`, `visa_expiry`,
   `vat_return`, `ct_return`, `esr_notification`, `ubo_update`, `audit_filing`, `custom`), title, due date,
   period label, recurrence (`none`, `monthly`, `quarterly`, `yearly`), status (`pending_review` → `open`
   → `met` | `waived`), confidence (`UNCERTAIN` until ops confirms the due date), evidence (file via the
   storage adapter, uploaded by the client or ops), checked by MKW. Generated by rule when an engagement
   completes (`packages/core/src/obligations.ts`: SET-* → licence renewal in 12 months; VISA-* → visa
   expiry in 24 months; VAT-RET → quarterly returns; CT-RET → yearly return; ESR-UBO → yearly UBO/ESR;
   AUD-EXT → yearly audit) as `pending_review`: ops confirms or edits the date before the client sees it.
   Ops may add a custom obligation. Daily job: reminders at 30 and 7 days and when overdue (client from
   Tasees, ops digest count). Marking met: evidence uploaded → `evidence_uploaded`; ops checks → `met`,
   and the next occurrence is created (pending review again only if the rule is UNCERTAIN; a confirmed
   recurring date rolls forward as `open`). Nothing is filed by the platform; the calendar tracks what a
   person did.

## Exclusions

Direct API integration with an accounting system (CSV first, owner names the system); Arabic (O9);
automatic filings or automatic statutory conclusions of any kind; obligations for partners (their licence
watch is O6).

## Acceptance evidence

- Tests: dashboard figures equal the database for a built period (invoices, payments, payouts, rates);
  ops sees no margin, finance does; previous period comparison; partner dashboard is its own firm's only;
  export CSV rows equal the database rows for the range, formula-safe, margin set 403 for ops, audited;
  obligations generated on completion as pending review and invisible to the client; ops confirm → client
  sees with the confidence label; reminders at 30 and 7 days once each and overdue once; evidence upload by
  client; met only after ops check; next occurrence created; waive with reason; custom obligation; other
  clients and partners denied at the API and database.
- Smoke: founder dashboard, accounting export download, client compliance calendar with an upload, ops
  calendar review; 1280 px and 390 px.

## Dependencies and owner decisions

- Accounting system name (column mapping); financial year end for corporate tax (default 31 December).

## Risks

- A wrong statutory date shown to a client: every generated date is UNCERTAIN until ops confirms it, the
  label stays on the client's screen, and the reminder says "check with MKW".
- Dashboard figures drifting from the books: figures come from the same invoice and payment rows the export
  uses, and the tests reconcile them.

## Rollback

Additive migration; dashboards and exports are read-only; the calendar job is a no-op without rows.
